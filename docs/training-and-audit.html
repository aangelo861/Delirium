17. Training and education

Staff groupMinimum training
Staff groupAll clinical staff in adult inpatient/ED areas (including HCAs, security)Minimum trainingDelirium awareness at induction and every 3 years: what delirium is, the 4AT, hypoactive delirium, the prevention bundle, de-escalation, MCA basics, and the Dementia vs Delirium session (Consultant Liaison Psychiatrist) or e-learning equivalent
Staff groupRegistered nurses, AHPsMinimum trainingAs above + competence in completing and interpreting the 4AT; post-medication monitoring
Staff groupDoctors (FY–consultant), ACPs, non-medical prescribersMinimum trainingAs above + this policy's pharmacological section, ECG/QTc interpretation, capacity assessment and best-interests documentation; RT training where applicable
Staff groupPharmacistsMinimum trainingMedication review for delirium; anticholinergic burden; this policy
Staff groupCritical care staffMinimum trainingCAM-ICU/ICDSC competence
Staff groupMCA/DoLS leads and link nursesMinimum trainingAdvanced MCA/DoLS/restraint

The teaching session Dementia vs Delirium (key messages: 4AT is quick and reliable; delirium = acute, fluctuating, often reversible; dementia = chronic, progressive; hypoactive delirium is commonly missed; always treat the underlying cause; communication with family is essential) forms the core of the Trust programme and its case scenarios (post-operative hypoactive delirium; night-time agitation in Alzheimer's disease; hallucinations with parkinsonism – why haloperidol must be avoided) map directly to sections 8, 12 and 13 of this policy.

18. Audit, monitoring and quality standards

The delirium working group will audit annually against NICE QS63 and this policy. Proposed indicators:

#Indicator (source)Standard
#1Indicator (source)At-risk adults (section 6) with a 4AT documented within 24 hours of admission (QS63 st.1; CG103 1.2/1.6)Standard≥ 90 %
#2Indicator (source)At-risk adults with a documented multicomponent prevention care plan within 24 hours (QS63 st.2)Standard≥ 90 %
#3Indicator (source)At-risk adults with a documented medication review within 24 hours (SIGN 157)Standard≥ 90 %
#4Indicator (source)Patients with delirium who received an antipsychotic/benzodiazepine with documented de-escalation attempts and indication (QS63 st.3)Standard100 %
#5Indicator (source)Haloperidol prescriptions with ECG/QTc documented before (or reason for deferral) and Parkinson's/DLB excludedStandard100 %
#6Indicator (source)Haloperidol cumulative dose > 2 mg/24 h in patients ≥ 65 with documented senior reviewStandard100 %
#7Indicator (source)Post-dose monitoring documented per 12.9Standard100 %
#8Indicator (source)Antipsychotic started for delirium and still prescribed at dischargeStandard≤ 5 %, all with stop/review date and named reviewer
#9Indicator (source)Patients/carers given written delirium information (QS63 st.4)Standard≥ 90 %
#10Indicator (source)Delirium recorded in the discharge summary and coded (QS63 st.5)Standard100 %
#11Indicator (source)Incidents (Datix) involving restraint, RT, absconding, falls with harm in patients with deliriumStandardReviewed quarterly
#12Indicator (source)Staff training compliance (section 17)Standard≥ 85 %

Results are reported to the Medicines Management Committee, the Patient Safety Committee and the Dementia and Delirium Steering Group [Trust to confirm].
