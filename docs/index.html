Delirium

Where do I start?

Choose the situation. A few questions lead to the right part of the policy.

  • A patient has new or fluctuating confusion, drowsiness or withdrawal, or is "not themselves"
  • A patient with delirium is distressed, agitated or a risk to themselves or others
  • Medication has been given for behavioural disturbance
  • Delirium is likely or confirmed and I need to find and treat the cause
  • A patient is at risk of delirium and I want to prevent it
  • I am planning discharge after delirium
  • A special situation: Parkinson's or Lewy body dementia, critical care, withdrawal, end of life, dementia, capacity

Immediate action: Acute cognitive change

Immediate safety assessment: ABCDE, observations and capillary glucose, before or alongside cognitive testing.

An acute change in cognition is a medical emergency until proven otherwise. Full immediate assessment (section 9.1)
