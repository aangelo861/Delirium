Drafting note

Information:

Drafting note. This document consolidates (a) the existing Trust draft Pharmacological management – Delirium guidelines, (b) the written feedback on that draft from the Consultant Liaison Psychiatrist (SG), all of which has been incorporated, and (c) the Dementia vs Delirium teaching material, and re-bases the whole on the current NICE guideline CG103 (last updated 18 January 2023), NICE Quality Standard QS63, SIGN 157, the MHRA Drug Safety Update on haloperidol (December 2021), NICE NG97 (dementia), NICE NG10 (violence and aggression), and the Mental Capacity Act 2005. Items marked [Trust to confirm] need a local decision before ratification.

Chelsea and Westminster Hospital NHS Foundation Trust
