Appendix D

Pharmacological management – one-page quick reference

SEVERE BEHAVIOURAL DISTURBANCE IN DELIRIUM – QUICK REFERENCE
===============================================================
STEP 1  Is there an unmet need / acute physical cause?  Pain · retention · constipation ·
        hypoxia · hypoglycaemia · withdrawal · fear.   → TREAT IT.
STEP 2  De-escalate: calm, one voice, reorientate, reduce stimulation, family, distract.
        Document attempts.  Enhanced observation. Falls plan.
STEP 3  Medication ONLY if de-escalation ineffective/not feasible AND patient is:
          • severely distressed; or
          • an immediate risk of physical harm to self/others; or
          • preventing essential investigation/treatment that cannot safely be delayed.
        NOT for: falls risk, wandering, calling out, night restlessness, hypoactive delirium.
STEP 4  Check: Parkinson's / DLB / parkinsonism?  ──YES──►  NO HALOPERIDOL.
                                                            Quetiapine 12.5–25 mg (specialist advice)
                                                            or lorazepam 0.5 mg. Senior review.
        Check: ECG / QTc  (>450 M, >460 F = prolonged; >500 = do not give haloperidol)
        Correct K+, Mg2+, Ca2+.  Review other QT-prolonging drugs.  Previous NMS? Recent MI? Arrhythmia?
        Capacity assessed & documented; best-interests decision if lacking capacity.
        Do not delay for ECG if IMMEDIATE serious risk – document and obtain ASAP.
STEP 5  HALOPERIDOL  0.25–0.5 mg ORAL (IM only if oral impossible/ineffective)
        Repeat ONLY after reassessment of mental state, obs, adverse effects – usually 30–60 min.
        ≥65 / frail: cumulative >2 mg/24 h needs senior (SpR/consultant) review.
        Usually ≤1 week.  Review indication DAILY.  >48 h anticipated → CoE consultant / Liaison Psychiatry.
   or   LORAZEPAM 0.5 mg PO/IM (oral first) – only for alcohol/benzo withdrawal, specialist advice,
        or where antipsychotics contraindicated/high risk after senior review.  Max 2 mg/24 h.
        Never IM lorazepam within 1 h of IM olanzapine.  Flumazenil available.
STEP 6  MONITOR & DOCUMENT: RR · SpO2 · BP · pulse · temp · consciousness (ACVPU) ·
        agitation/distress · adverse effects incl. EPS.  15–30 min post dose then hourly
        until no concerns (q15 min if asleep/oversedated, IM, max exceeded, restrained).
        REPEAT OBS BEFORE ANY FURTHER DOSE.  Procyclidine available for dystonia.
STEP 7  REVIEW DAILY.  STOP as soon as clinically appropriate.  Do NOT continue at
        discharge unless exceptional – then stop/review date + named reviewer.
        Covert medication → Trust Covert Medication Policy + best-interests decision only.
        Restraint / deprivation of liberty → MCA s.6 proportionality; DoLS urgent authorisation.
