Prevention – multicomponent intervention

Delivered by a multidisciplinary team trained and competent in delirium prevention, tailored to the individual's needs and care setting, and started within 24 hours of admission (NICE CG103 1.4). Ensure the same team cares for the person where possible and avoid moving people within and between wards or rooms unless absolutely necessary (CG103 1.4.1–1.4.2).

DomainActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)
DomainCognitive impairment / disorientationActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Appropriate lighting and clear signage; a clock (24-hour in critical care) and calendar visible; re-orientate – explain where they are, who you are, what your role is; cognitively stimulating activities (e.g. reminiscence); facilitate regular visits from family and friends (open/extended visiting for carers).
DomainDehydration / constipationActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Encourage oral fluids; fluid balance chart; consider subcutaneous or IV fluids if necessary; take advice on fluid balance in comorbidities (e.g. heart failure, CKD); bowel chart and management of constipation.
DomainHypoxiaActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Assess for hypoxia; optimise oxygen saturation if clinically appropriate.
DomainInfectionActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Look for and treat infection; avoid unnecessary catheterisation; infection-control procedures.
DomainImmobility / limited mobilityActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Mobilise soon after surgery/admission; encourage walking (with walking aids where needed and accessible); range-of-motion exercises for those who cannot walk.
DomainPainActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Assess for pain – look for non-verbal signs, particularly in people who cannot communicate (e.g. Abbey Pain Scale, PAINAD); start and review appropriate pain management.
DomainMedicationActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Review all medicines for type and number; rationalise; minimise anticholinergic burden (see 10.3), opioids, benzodiazepines and other sedatives; check for withdrawal risk.
DomainPoor nutritionActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Follow the Trust nutrition policy; ensure dentures fit properly and are used; assistance with meals; red trays / protected mealtimes.
DomainSensory impairmentActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Resolve reversible causes (e.g. impacted ear wax); ensure hearing aids and glasses are available, working and worn.
DomainSleep disturbanceActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Avoid nursing or medical procedures during sleeping hours if possible; schedule medication rounds to avoid disturbing sleep; reduce noise and light at night; avoid sedative-hypnotics.
DomainBladder and bowelActions (NICE CG103 1.4.3–1.4.10; SIGN 157)Regular toileting; avoid catheters where possible; screen for retention.

Antipsychotics and other drugs must not be used prophylactically to prevent delirium (SIGN 157 – insufficient evidence of benefit; benzodiazepines markedly increase the odds of delirium).

Consider the use of a structured ward-level prevention bundle (e.g. the NHS Scotland TIME bundle – Think/exclude triggers, Investigate and intervene, Management plan, Engage and explore – or an equivalent Trust care plan) [Trust to confirm local bundle].
